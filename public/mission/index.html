Scaling Compute

AI leadership requires growth in compute capacity and electricity production.  Advanced or “Gen IV” Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), running on High Assay Low Enriched (HALEU), will power that growth from behind the meter and on the grid.

Advancing Manufacturing

Our manufacturing sector runs on reliable, affordable grid power.  Our civilian reactor fleet, fueled by Low Enriched Uranium (LEU), provides almost 20% of today’s grid power and almost half our emissions-free energy.

Expanding Frontiers

Operating in remote, harsh and austere environments requires steady, compact, low-maintenance power. Containerized microreactors, fueled by HALEU with long refueling cycles, are an ideal fit.

America needs power.

Power to accelerate growth and increase prosperity. Power to advance manufacturing, scale compute, and expand frontiers. Power to end reliance on foreign adversaries.

The future is nuclear.

Nuclear power is the only source of electricity that is reliable, scalable, and clean. It is the single safest source of baseload energy, and it doesn’t breathe our air or mar our landscapes. It is the most perfect form of energy ever discovered.

America pioneered nuclear power.

Like flight, space travel, and computers, nuclear energy was developed here in the US. From reactors to fuel, we were once the world leader. But we stopped building new reactors. We outsourced fuel production. We became dependent on countries who seek to surpass us. 

We gave up our lead.

Our only limitations were self-imposed. Physics showed a path, but bureaucracy blocked it and decades of timidity abandoned it. Human will is clearing the path here in the US once more. 

We will lead again.

Our team shares a rare and specific talent: breaking into stagnant sectors and unlocking their potential. While others have shipped products, our team has shipped industries.

It is a new era in American power.

Domestic nuclear energy will power an era of prosperity, safety, and self-sufficiency. All we need lies beneath our feet, in the bedrock of our country, ready to power our communities for generations.

America’s potential is written in stone.

We turn it into power.
