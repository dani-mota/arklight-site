■ THE VISION

A training center where students build the real thing.

Most kids never touch the work that runs the world. We're changing that: facility tours inside real factories, hands-on build challenges with real tools, and technical learning taught by working engineers. We built the model for school districts. Homeschool families kept asking for a way in, so we're piloting one in Austin.
