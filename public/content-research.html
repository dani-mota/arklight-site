The U.S. skilled
labor shortage.

Up to 2.1 million manufacturing jobs could go unfilled by 2030. Built bottom-up with the Arklight Demand Model, the gap resolves trade by trade: roughly 87,000 unfilled electrician seats a year, 40,000 machinists, 48,000 fabricators. Reshoring is widening it, not closing it. This is the overview that hubs every briefing below.
