What Trade School 2.0 Is

A Factory and Training Institution in One — Software-Defined.

The contract is the curriculum. The factory floor is the classroom. The part is the test.

AI-Adaptive Learning Engine
An AI tutor delivers the exact math, physics, and engineering each student needs — anchored to the real contract they're about to execute. Theory is never abstract. It's always the next job.
Live Production Floor
Students produce real parts on real contracts from Day 1. Graduated pressure. Real quality audits. Real stakes. Not a simulation — production.
Continuous Assessment
Every decision, every reasoning pattern, every performance metric — captured across the full program. Not a final exam. A year of verified evidence no institution has ever produced.
Arklight OS
One platform binds learning, production, and assessment into a single closed loop. Failure on a contract automatically reroutes learning. Mastery automatically advances the student. No gaps. No wasted time.
