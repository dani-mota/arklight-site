Fabricators
labor shortage.

The metal-fabrication skill cluster — welders, structural iron & steel workers, structural metal fabricators/fitters, and layout workers/shipfitters — employs ~600,000 nationally. A job-ready supply of ~37,000/yr meets demand of ~85,000/yr, a structurally widening shortfall. Demand triangulated three independent ways (mission top-down, pipeline bottom-up, market signal); government data sets the replacement floor only. Modeled trajectories are flagged.

Sources & method

Demand triangulated three independent ways: (A) mission top-down — Navy 174K shipyard workers/decade (DOD 45-Day Review via GAO-25-106286) × ~15.5% metal-fab cluster share of NAICS 336600 (BLS OEWS), plus Submarine Industrial Base 140K, CHIPS fab construction, IIJA, IRA, converted to annual cluster flows over the BLS replacement floor (~62K/yr); (B) pipeline bottom-up — NCES IPEDS (CIP 48.0508 welding ~50,561 completions 2023) + DOL RAPIDS + military (HT/SW, MOS 1316/91E) + ATDM, discounted for completion (~72%) and day-one readiness (~65%, per McKinsey Jan 2026 maritime study); (C) market signal — AWS ~82,500 openings/yr, HII Ingalls Mar-2026 wage hike (+18% immediate / +35–47% to 2031), Electric Boat 33K peak workforce. Methods A and C independently converge at ~80–90K/yr. Cost-of-vacancy uses BEA value-added per worker; multipliers NAM/IMPLAN ~5× manufacturing, MARAD 3.67× shipbuilding.

Modeled trajectories (panels 02, 04) flagged in-panel. China ~200× shipbuilding capacity is un-triangulated on exact magnitude. Treat range and direction as robust; any single point as directional. — Full sourcing in the companion brief.

After decades of offshoring and decline, capital and ambition are flooding back into American industry, across manufacturing, energy, defense, and AI.

But you cannot build what you cannot staff.

The workforce that built America's industrial base is retiring, and nothing has replaced it. The people who know how to make things are disappearing.

We built the arsenal of democracy. We put people on the moon. The skill to make hard things was forged here, on real floors, by real operators.

Then we stopped.

We told a generation the only path was a four-year degree and a mountain of debt. We let the trades wither and the pipeline run dry.

We will build the builders.

Arklight develops the talent to rebuild American industry. Real operators, trained on real work, with military methodology and AI, ready to ship on day one.

It is a new era of American making.

Give a generation the chance to build, and they will rebuild the country. The capacity to make what we need, and the people who can make it.
