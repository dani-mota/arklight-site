## Your task: pull the questions people ask about our topics from AnswerThePublic

**Context.** Project Arklight trains and deploys skilled industrial workers (machinists, welders, fabricators, technicians) and publishes research on the U.S. skilled-labor shortage. We're doing **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** — getting our pages cited when people ask AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini) about these topics. Answer engines answer *questions*, so we need the real questions people ask. AnswerThePublic surfaces exactly those.

**What I need from you:** run a list of keywords through AnswerThePublic and send back the **Questions** and **Comparisons** it returns for each. That's it — I'll turn them into on-page answers.

**Time:** ~30–45 minutes.

### Tool & access
- Go to **answerthepublic.com** (Neil Patel's tool) and log into the Project Arklight account.

### One-time setup (do this first)
- Open **Keyword Coverage** in ATP and add our sitemap: `https://www.arklight.us/sitemap.xml`
- This makes ATP hide topics we already cover and show only real gaps.

### For each seed keyword below
1. Type the keyword into ATP and run the search.
2. Copy (or export) these two sections:
   - **Questions** — the wheel of who / what / why / how / when / which / can / is / are / do
   - **Comparisons** — the "X vs Y" / "X or Y" section
3. Skip the "Alphabeticals" and "Prepositions" wheels — lower value for this.
4. If ATP puts an **"Opportunity"** badge or an **"AI visibility"** flag on the keyword, note it — those are priorities.

### Seed keywords (grouped; do them top to bottom)

**Group 1 — Careers (highest priority)**
`industrial operator` · `machine operator` · `machinist` · `CNC machinist` · `welder` · `metal fabricator` · `electrician apprentice`

**Group 2 — Money (highest priority)**
`machinist salary` · `welder salary` · `manufacturing jobs no degree` · `highest paying trades`

**Group 3 — Employer-side**
`hire machinists` · `hire manufacturing workers` · `industrial talent pipeline`

**Group 4 — Training / school**
`trade school` · `apprenticeship` · `trade school vs community college`

**Group 5 — Themes**
`skilled labor shortage` · `reshoring jobs` · `veteran manufacturing jobs`

**Group 6 — Employer / workforce development (B2B buyer side)**
`manufacturing workforce development` · `workforce development program` · `defense industrial base workforce` · `apprenticeship program for manufacturers` · `how to scale manufacturing hiring` · `skilled trades staffing`

*(If short on time, Groups 1, 2, and 6 are the priority — 1 and 2 are candidate demand, 6 is buyer demand. Do the rest if you can.)*

### How to send it back (format matters — please follow this)
For each seed, paste the lists like this, so it's easy to process:

```
## SEED: machinist
### Questions
- how much do machinists make
- is machinist a good career
- what does a machinist do
- ...
### Comparisons
- machinist vs welder
- machinist vs cnc operator
- ...
### Flags
- "machinist salary" — Best for AI Visibility
```

A CSV export attached per seed is also fine — just label which seed it's for.

### Done when
- All seed keywords in Groups 1–2 are run and their Questions + Comparisons are sent back (Groups 3–5 are a bonus).
- Any AI-visibility / opportunity flags are noted.

Thank you — this directly feeds what we publish next.
