## The signature that tells you WHO to sell (the pattern across all accounts)
The hottest buyers share four traits. Score any account on these:
1. **A fresh / greenfield production-site ramp** with a dated public trigger (new plant, contract, shipyard).
2. **ITAR / US-person gate** on the reqs → shrinks the hireable pool to US persons (acute scarcity).
3. **No — or immature — in-house training academy** (they can't self-serve the pipeline).
4. **"Will train / no experience required / production at scale" language in their own reqs** → proof they can't find experienced people and are willing to train = Arklight's exact opening.

Accounts with all four = hot. Accounts that already built their own academy (Tesla MDP/START, Hadrian's 30-day model) = harder — sell *overflow*, not core.
