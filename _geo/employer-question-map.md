## THE ONE FINDING (all 4 agents converged independently)
There is **no neutral, buyable answer** to the buyer's real question:
> **"How do I get permanent, production-ready operators at ramp scale — without staffing-agency markup/churn, and without standing up my own multi-year, DOL-registered apprenticeship?"**

The market is fragmented and every incumbent answers only *part* of it:
- **Staffing agencies** rent bodies (45-55% markup; churn temps every 90 days so you can't convert them).
- **Community colleges** deliver credentials, not production-ready operators (only ~62% of employers think grads are work-ready).
- **LMS / upskilling vendors** deliver content, not people.
- **Registered apprenticeship** delivers people but slowly (2-5 yrs, DOL paperwork, mentor-hours).
- The only "trained in weeks" voice online is **Hadrian's own PR** (proprietary, not for sale).

**Arklight's category = the unclaimed third door: train-and-deploy production-ready operators to your line, at ramp speed.** This should be the flagship pillar. Every content asset below ladders up to it.

## THE #1 OBJECTION (surfaced in all 4 reports)
**"Why train them if they'll just leave?" (poaching).** Pre-empt it everywhere. Counter with (a) retention data ("only ~7% leave within a year of completing training"; "42% stay 5+ years"; apprenticeship ROI $1.44 median per $1) and (b) the reframe: **own the pipeline, not the person.** Pair with the defense-specific angle ("why defense manufacturers can't keep the talent they train").
