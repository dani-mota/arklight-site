# ATP Seed & Question Strategy — B2B DIB (two personas)
*Companion to `icp-buyer-personas.md`. Canonical seed map for the GEO/SEO loop. Created 2026-07.*

## Philosophy: value-first, customer-centric
We do not seed ATP with our solution ("training program," "workforce partner," "Arklight"). We seed with the **buyer's problem, in the buyer's words, at each stage of their job-to-be-done.** The test for every resulting asset: *does this help them do their job, make their case, or reduce their risk?* If it only sells us, it fails. These buyers are exhausted by vendor hype and respond only to a peer, evidence-first, no-hype register. Value earns the trust; trust + being the sole credible source on their exact question earns the lead. Value is the conversion strategy, not a step before it.

## How to read this
Each **seed** is what you type into Answer the Public. ATP explodes it into questions (how/why/what/vs/can), prepositions, and comparisons. For each job-cluster below: the **seeds** to run, the **high-value questions** ATP should surface, the **value we provide** (what *they* get), and the **content** it maps to (existing page to sharpen, or gap to build).

Prioritize by **intent + persona-fit + zero-competition — never volume.** Buyer volume is ~0-50/mo; that is expected and irrelevant. A 20-search question a Head of Talent asks before a vendor eval beats 5,000 candidate searches.

# PERSONA A — Head of Talent
*Strategic, fear-driven. Already suspects building beats buying; fears owning the bet. Value = evidence that relieves the competence-anxiety + confidence/language to make and defend the decision.*

### Job A1 — Name & validate the pain *(top funnel — "is this me, or is it structural?")*
- **Seeds:** `manufacturing labor shortage` · `skilled labor shortage` · `skilled trades shortage` · `manufacturing workforce shortage` · `can't find skilled workers` · `machinist shortage` · `welder shortage` · `defense manufacturing workforce shortage`
- **Questions ATP surfaces:** why is there a manufacturing labor shortage · how bad is the skilled labor shortage · will the labor shortage get worse · what is causing the skilled trades shortage · is the machinist shortage real
- **Value we provide:** proof that the gap is *structural, not their failure*, quantified with the Arklight Demand Model. This directly relieves the competence-anxiety ("effort doesn't move it") — it tells them the pool genuinely doesn't exist.
- **Content:** SHARPEN the shortage briefs (`/research/*`), the pillar (`/research/skilled-labor-shortage`), the dataset. Match the exact question phrasings as H2s/FAQ.
- **Payload:** "It's not you. It's structural. Here's the evidence."

### Job A2 — Quantify it for the board *(mid — "I need a number to defend the people plan")*
- **Seeds:** `cost of unfilled manufacturing jobs` · `cost of open positions` · `cost of employee turnover manufacturing` · `manufacturing job vacancy cost` · `economic impact of labor shortage`
- **Questions:** how much does an unfilled position cost · what is the cost of vacancy · how to calculate the cost of not hiring · cost of turnover in manufacturing
- **Value:** the number + the method to defend the workforce plan in a board deck. Makes them credible upstairs — a gift they'll use in front of their CEO.
- **Content:** the dataset (cost-of-vacancy) + methodology. GAP: *"The real cost of not staffing your ramp."*
- **Payload:** "Here's the number you put in the deck."

### Job A3 — Evaluate build vs buy *(mid — the structural fork)*
- **Seeds:** `build vs buy talent` · `build vs buy workforce` · `staffing agency vs in-house training` · `should we use a staffing agency` · `how to build a talent pipeline` · `workforce development vs staffing agency`
- **Questions:** is it better to build or buy talent · staffing agency vs building your own pipeline · how to build a manufacturing talent pipeline · when should you build vs buy workforce
- **Value:** an honest comparison *with the math* — including the case against us where it's true. Helps them make and own the decision they already lean toward.
- **Content:** SHARPEN `/content/build-buy-rent-industrial-workforce` (their exact dilemma), `/content/manufacturing-workforce-development`, `/hire`.
- **Payload:** "De-risk the bet. Be the leader who fixed it."

### Job A4 — De-risk the build *(mid/bottom — the REAL obstacle: fear of owning a second bet)*
- **Seeds:** `how to build a workforce pipeline` · `how long to build a talent pipeline` · `workforce development ROI` · `how to reduce time to productivity` · `risks of building a training program`
- **Questions:** how long does it take to build a talent pipeline · how to measure workforce development ROI · how fast can you train a machinist · what if our training program fails
- **Value:** show the build is *faster and smaller* than they fear — military-derived, competency-measured, production-ready in under a year. This is the single most conversion-critical value: it removes the fear that blocks the yes.
- **Content:** GAP: *"How to de-risk building your workforce pipeline."* Supported by `measuring-competency`, `build-manufacturing-apprenticeship-program`, `/trade-school`.
- **Payload:** "The bet is smaller and faster than you think."

### Job A5 — The DIB constraint *(mid — their unique, isolating reality)*
- **Seeds:** `ITAR cleared workforce` · `clearable talent` · `US person requirement manufacturing` · `security clearance manufacturing shortage` · `defense manufacturing labor shortage` · `reshoring workforce shortage`
- **Questions:** what is a clearable workforce · how to hire cleared manufacturing workers · why is defense manufacturing short on labor · what does ITAR mean for hiring
- **Value:** name the ITAR/comp-war wedge precisely and show how to build a *clearable* pipeline. Signals "we understand your actual constraint" — the peer-who-gets-it they lack.
- **Content:** SHARPEN `/content/clearable-manufacturing-talent`; the DIB briefs (submarine, CHIPS, Anduril, Coherent).
- **Payload:** "We get your real constraint, not the generic one."

# The value test & register (guardrails on every asset)
1. **The value test:** does this help them *do their job, make their case, or reduce their risk*? If it only sells Arklight, cut it.
2. **Register:** peer-to-peer, evidence-first, no hype, no adjectives without a number. The only voice that survives their earned skepticism.
3. **Protagonist rule:** make the buyer the hero who fixed/built it. Never imply they failed and need rescuing.
4. **Persona A gets evidence + de-risking; Persona B gets method + speed.** Never sell B on "why build"; never hand A hype instead of proof.
5. **The missing input ATP can't give:** verbatim fear-language from real discovery calls. Feed transcripts back into `icp-buyer-personas.md` as they happen — that is the highest-value upgrade to this whole map.
